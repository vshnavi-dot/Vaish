About

Every review is a product I bought or was gifted and actually finished.

I started reviewing skincare because I couldn't tell which "holy grail" claims were real and which were just good lighting. Six years and a few hundred empty bottles later, the rule hasn't changed: if I haven't used it down to the last squeeze, it doesn't get rated.

I cover skincare, makeup, haircare and fragrance — mostly what's actually sitting on Indian shelves, at prices that matter, reviewed with the same routine every time.

The honesty policy: No brand pays for placement or a rating. If something's sent to me for free, it's marked "gifted" — never "sponsored."

Rating Guide

Five swatches, not five stars.

Every product gets rated the way you'd judge a shade on your hand — by how much of it actually gets used. Here's what each swatch level means before you read a single review.

Hard pass

Sat half-used in a drawer. Wouldn't buy again or recommend.

Meh

Did the bare minimum. Fine for the price, forgettable otherwise.

It's fine

Works as promised. Solid backup, not a first reach.

Repurchase

Already reordered it. Would recommend without a big caveat.

Holy grail

Rebought more than once. Recommended to actual friends and family.
